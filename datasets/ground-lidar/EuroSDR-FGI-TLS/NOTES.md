# EuroSDR/FGI TLS benchmark notes

- **LiDAR sensor model:** The benchmark paper identifies RIEGL VZ-400 TLS data. [Part I paper](http://eurosdr.net/sites/default/files/uploaded_files/eurosdr_publication_ndeg_71_part_1.pdf)
- **IMU included:** No separate IMU stream is described for this static TLS benchmark. [EuroSDR project page](https://www.eurosdr.net/research/project/benchmarking-terrestrial-laser-scanning-forestry-applications)
- **Continuous walking or driving sequences:** No. The benchmark uses static single-scan and multi-scan plot configurations, not continuous rover traversals. [Part I paper](http://eurosdr.net/sites/default/files/uploaded_files/eurosdr_publication_ndeg_71_part_1.pdf)
- **Point-cloud format:** XYZ and intensity data are described for the benchmark; exact container extension is **NOT STATED IN SOURCES**. [EuroSDR project page](https://www.eurosdr.net/research/project/benchmarking-terrestrial-laser-scanning-forestry-applications)
- **Labels:** The benchmark asks participants for tree position, DBH, stem diameters, and DTM outputs, but provided point-level ground/tree/shrub/stem labels are **NOT STATED IN SOURCES**. [EuroSDR project page](https://www.eurosdr.net/research/project/benchmarking-terrestrial-laser-scanning-forestry-applications)
- **Region and forest type:** Southern boreal Finland and 24 plots of 32 m by 32 m are described in the benchmark materials. [Part I paper](http://eurosdr.net/sites/default/files/uploaded_files/eurosdr_publication_ndeg_71_part_1.pdf)
- **Licence:** **NOT STATED IN SOURCES**; the project page describes a benchmark and contact-based participation rather than a dataset licence. [EuroSDR project page](https://www.eurosdr.net/research/project/benchmarking-terrestrial-laser-scanning-forestry-applications)
- **Download size:** **NOT STATED IN SOURCES**. [EuroSDR project page](https://www.eurosdr.net/research/project/benchmarking-terrestrial-laser-scanning-forestry-applications)

Paper: [International benchmarking Part I](https://researchportal.helsinki.fi/en/publications/international-benchmarking-of-terrestrial-laser-scanning-approach-3/).
