# Weiser et al. German forest LiDAR notes

- **LiDAR sensor model:** The three platforms use RIEGL VQ-780i (airborne), RIEGL miniVUX-1UAV (UAV), and RIEGL VZ-400 (terrestrial), as described in the paper. [ESSD paper](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/14/2989/2022/)
- **IMU included:** **NOT STATED IN SOURCES** as a separately downloadable IMU stream. The dataset provides platform point clouds and derived tree products; an IMU used by a platform is not proof that raw IMU data are included. [PANGAEA record](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.942856) [ESSD paper](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/14/2989/2022/)
- **Continuous walking or driving sequences:** No continuous walking/driving sequence is described. Airborne, UAV, and tripod campaigns were acquired over forest plots in 2019–2020. [PANGAEA record](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.942856)
- **Point-cloud format:** **NOT STATED IN SOURCES** on the accessed PANGAEA landing page; it provides platform point clouds, single-tree point clouds, and tree metrics. [PANGAEA record](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.942856)
- **Labels:** Field inventory includes species, height, DBH, crown base height, and crown diameter; point clouds are matched to those measurements. Ground, shrub, and stem point labels are **NOT STATED IN SOURCES**. [PANGAEA record](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.942856)
- **Region and forest type:** Mixed central European forests near Bretten and Karlsruhe, Baden-Württemberg, Germany; plots were acquired under leaf-on conditions, with UAV also under leaf-off conditions. [PANGAEA record](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.942856)
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0. [PANGAEA record](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.942856)
- **Download size:** **NOT STATED IN SOURCES** on the accessed landing page. [PANGAEA record](https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.942856)

Paper: [ESSD article](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-14-2989-2022). Code/data tools: [SYSSIFOSS Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/6759914).
